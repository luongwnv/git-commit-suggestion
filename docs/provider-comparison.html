Authoritative state of each provider class in src/providers/. Keep this in sync with config/providers.yml.

When to use what

  • You want it to just work with zero setup: auto (default). Tries 4 providers in turn.
  • You want quality, free, no key: pollinations on openai-large.
  • You want frontier models without a key: duckduckgo with o3-mini or claude-haiku.
  • You want speed and free with a key: Groq with llama-3.1-8b-instant.
  • You want offline / privacy: Ollama with a local model.
  • You have a paid OpenAI/Anthropic key already: use that — best quality.
  • You explicitly accept the risks of unofficial reverse proxies: g4f.
